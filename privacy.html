Privacy policy for Quire

Livewire Limitless
Last updated: 7 October 2026

This policy covers the Quire Android app (package com.kibalivewire.quire), published by Livewire Limitless. It describes what the app stores on your device and what, if anything, leaves it.

Quire is a notebook. Pages, sheets, folders, and pictures you insert stay on the device. Livewire Limitless does not run an account system for Quire and does not receive a copy of your notebook.

What stays on the device

  • The notebook, including text, sheets, folders, and pictures you add.
  • A passcode, if you set one. It is stored only as a local screen gate. It is not an account and it is not sent to us.
  • Android automatic backup of the app is turned off.

Camera and photos

The camera and photo library are used only when you choose to insert a picture. Livewire Limitless does not upload those pictures. A picture leaves the device only if you export, share, or transfer it yourself.

Sharing you start

Export, backup, and Transfer to another device build a file, including a zip, and hand it to Android’s share sheet or to a place you pick. That file is not encrypted by Quire. Whoever you send it to can read it. We do not receive that file.

What is sent off the device

Nothing is sent while you write, save, or turn pages. If you ask Quill to polish grammar, look up a word, or fetch a synonym or definition, only that word or a short passage is sent, and only to these services:

  • LanguageTool (api.languagetool.org), for a grammar check of the passage you asked to polish. The text sent is limited to a short excerpt.
  • Datamuse (api.datamuse.com), for synonyms, related words, and pronunciation of the word you asked about.
  • Wiktionary (en.wiktionary.org), for a definition of the word you asked about.

Those services are independent of Livewire Limitless. They receive the word or passage needed to answer the request, and their own privacy policies apply to that request. Quire does not send your notebook, your passcode, or your pictures to them.

A web search, if you open one, is a DuckDuckGo page in the browser. That search is between you and DuckDuckGo.

What we do not do

We do not sell your data. We do not use advertising. We do not add analytics that identify you. We do not share notebook contents with other companies, except for the word and grammar requests above, which go only to the services named there.

Children

Quire is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated page will be posted at this same address with a new date.
